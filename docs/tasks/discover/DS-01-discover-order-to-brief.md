# DS-01 Discover module order, against the brief

- **Status:** decision
- **Type:** decision
- **Depends on:** none
- **Design:** `docs/design/screens/discover-guest-brief.md`, section 11.2 (guest Discover order).
- **Now:** Built in the prototype's order: Promoted places, Happening soon, Discover by city, Guided tours, Places with experiences, Communities organising things, Recent moments, Public bucket lists.

## Done when

- [ ] Owner confirms the order to build
- [ ] The page is reordered, or the difference is recorded as deliberate

## Decision

The brief's order is: 1 location, search and quick filters; 2 things to do
soon; 3 public bucket lists; 4 communities to explore; 5 what's happening at
places; 6 moments; 7 explore further (cities, itineraries, collections).

Against that, our Discover has bucket lists at 8 and communities at 6. The brief
says "Bucket Lists and Communities must be among the first three substantive
content modules". Promoted places has no place in the brief's order.

This contradicts the prototype we have been building to. The brief governs.
